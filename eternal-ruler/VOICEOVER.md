## Read this before you record

**Record cue by cue, not in one take.** The app stores each line separately and plays
it at the right moment on its own, so it does the syncing for you. A single continuous
take would have to be cut up anyway, and any line you dislike could not be redone
without re-recording the ones around it.

**Where it goes:** in the app, **Guide Voice → Your own voice → The opening**. Each cue
below has an id matching a row on that screen. Anything you do not record falls back to
the chosen voice, so you can do one line, or all nine, in any order.

**Timings are targets, not limits.** The app waits for you to finish before moving on,
so running long changes nothing except the total length. They are here so the whole
thing lands near two minutes and sits with the music rather than fighting it.

**How to sound like a person:** speak to one person in a room, not to an audience. Use
contractions. Do not over-enunciate. Leave the small breaths in — they are the single
biggest difference between a recording and a read. Record somewhere soft, a couple of
hand-widths from the mic, slightly off-axis so the plosives miss it.
