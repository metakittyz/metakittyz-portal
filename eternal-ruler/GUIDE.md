# The Framework

How to build a practice with this app. Five layers, one loop, one step a day.

**The whole point:** connect with your higher self, and become the eternal ruler of your own reality.
That line sits under the nav on every screen, next to your stage and day, so you never lose the thread.

---

## The loop

```
GROUND → REACH → DISCERN → BUILD → RETURN → (repeat)
```

Run it in order. Each layer fails without the one before it.

| Layer | Do | Room | Skip it and |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1 · Ground** | Sleep, light, movement, food | Intake | You hear nothing and blame yourself |
| **2 · Reach** | Address it. Ask once. Wait. | Reconnect | Nothing to work with |
| **3 · Discern** | Guidance, or fear in its coat? | Discernment Test | You follow the loudest voice |
| **4 · Build** | Identity, minimum, dates | Forge | Insight that never becomes Tuesday |
| **5 · Return** | Ground, eat, tell a person | Grounding | You come apart beautifully |

**Ground is not the warm-up.** It's a third of the work. Most people who hear nothing are just tired.

**Return is not optional.** Traditions that open people without teaching the way down produce casualties.

## Intake

What you feed the machine, in five channels: **content · food & drink · movement · substances · people**, plus sleep.

Log the thing, then tap what it did:

| | | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `−−` Drained | `−` Cost me | `0` Neutral |
| `+` Fed me | `++` Lit me up | |

That charge is the whole point — a food diary tells you what you ate, this tells you what it did.

**Patterns** ranks what repeatedly feeds you and what repeatedly costs you, then lines your net daily
charge up against your Readings: *your heat on fed days vs drained days*. Association, not proof — but
it's your own data, so you already know what else was going on.

## Daily build

Pick a size. Any size counts.

| | Time | Do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Minimum** | 3 min | Today's step, or one Reading |
| **Standard** | 15 min | The step + one journal entry |
| **Deep** | 45 min | Step, Reading, journal, one protocol |

The minimum on a bad day beats the deep version abandoned in week two.

## The key

Two numbers per domain: how **you** feel, how your **higher self** feels. The gap is the reading.

| Gap | Verdict | Means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| +25 or more | **Calling** | Something's waiting. An invitation. |
| −25 or more | **Overreach** | Want, not purpose. Whose fire is this? |
| Both hot, close | **Strength** | A pillar. Anchor here. |
| Both cold, close | **Numb** | Not yours, or frozen deep. |
| Otherwise | **Steady** | Fine. Leave it. |

You're rating your *belief* about your higher self. If every number comes out harsh, that's the finding.

## Five rules

1. **It moves when you do.** Position is steps completed, never dates. You cannot fall behind.
2. **One step a day.** Tomorrow beats three today.
3. **Sleep down → practice down.** Not up. Choosing less is discernment.
4. **Skippable means skippable.** Deep steps are marked. Nothing later depends on them.
5. **Ground after you open.** Every time.

## Stop signs

Reduce intensity and talk to a professional if you notice:

- Sleep going
- A persistent sense things are unreal
- Fear that won't settle
- Pulling away from people who know you
- Certainty that can't be questioned
- Big irreversible decisions made on guidance
- Someone who loves you says you seem different

Take the last one more seriously than the signs.

**In danger right now:** US & Canada **988** · UK & Ireland **116 123** · elsewhere your local emergency number or [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com).
