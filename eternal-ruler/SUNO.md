# The Overture — Suno voice-over prompts

> Generated by `npm run vo` from `src/lib/overture-scenes.js`. Regenerate rather than edit.

## Be warned about one thing first

Suno is a **music** generator. Asking it for dry spoken word is working against what it
does, and it will often sing anyway, add a backing bed you did not ask for, or fall into a
rhythmic cadence. Expect to regenerate several times per cue and to keep maybe one take in
three. If that gets tiring, the two reliable routes are still your own voice through the
Import button, or the OpenAI voice the app already has wired.

Also: the app plays **one file per line**, not one long take. So generate the nine short
cues below rather than the continuous version, unless you intend to cut it up yourself.

---

## Settings

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Instrumental | **off** |
| Style influence | high — you want it obeying "no singing" |
| Weirdness | low |

**Style prompt** — paste as-is:

```
spoken word narration, female voice, soft and intimate, close-mic, warm low register, unhurried, conversational, no singing, no melody, no rhythm, dry vocal, barely-there ambient drone underneath, cinematic, contemplative
```

**Exclude styles** — the half that does the real work:

```
singing, sung vocals, melody, autotune, harmony, choir, drums, percussion, beat, rap, rhyme, chorus, instrumental break, reverb wash, music swell, upbeat, bright
```

The `[Spoken Word]` tag at the top of every lyric block matters. Without it Suno reads the
box as song lyrics and sings them. Line breaks are phrasing — leave them where they are.

## The nine cues

One generation each. Download the take you like, then in the app:
**Guide Voice → Your own voice → The opening → ↑ Import** on the matching row.

### Cue 1 · `overture:matter`

Target 8.3s. Open quiet. This is the first thing anyone hears — underplay it. Statement of fact, not a reveal.

```
[Spoken Word]
[female narrator, soft, close, unhurried]

Nothing in you has ever been still.

Not one atom. Not for one second of your life.
```

### Cue 2 · `overture:light`

Target 11.4s. A little warmer. You are pointing at something, not explaining it. Let 'red' and 'blue' land separately.

```
[Spoken Word]
[female narrator, soft, close, unhurried]

Everything you have ever seen was a speed.

Slow waves look red. Fast ones look blue. That is the whole difference.
```

### Cue 3 · `overture:ocean`

Target 7.8s. Plain. The second sentence is the payoff, so leave a real gap before it.

```
[Spoken Word]
[female narrator, soft, close, unhurried]

Nothing overwhelming is ever one thing.

This whole sea is three simple waves, stacked.
```

### Cue 4 · `overture:heart`

Target 7.5s. Softest beat in the piece. Slow down. This one is about them, and they will feel it if you rush.

```
[Spoken Word]
[female narrator, soft, close, unhurried]

You have kept time your entire life.

About once a second, since before you were born.
```

### Cue 5 · `overture:everything`

Target 10.7s. Weight. The list of four is four separate items — comma pauses, not a run. Then stop hard before the last two words.

```
[Spoken Word]
[female narrator, soft, close, unhurried]

You are not in the world. You are made of its motion.

Matter, light, water, blood. Not like waves. Waves.
```

### Cue 6 · `overture:resonance`

Target 16.1s. The turn. First sentence flat and a bit cold, second one opens up. The last sentence is the thesis of the app — say it like you mean it, not like you are quoting it.

```
[Spoken Word]
[female narrator, soft, close, unhurried]

Nothing changes but agreement. And everything changes.

Out of step, two waves cancel to nothing. In step, the same two double. Connection was never about being close.
```

### Cue 7 · `overture:senses`

Target 11.0s. This is someone else's line, so let it sound borrowed — a shade lighter, almost like you are passing it on. Small pause before 'and we have guides.'

```
[Spoken Word]
[female narrator, soft, close, unhurried]

Birds have radar, bats have sonar — and we have guides.

Every creature is given a way to sense what it otherwise couldn't.
```

### Cue 8 · `overture:recognition`

Target 9.9s. Intimate. Nearly a whisper but not breathy. 'Nothing arrives' can be almost thrown away; 'you come into range' is the one to land.

```
[Spoken Word]
[female narrator, soft, close, unhurried]

Nothing arrives. You come into range.

The faint ones were always there, the whole time you could not hear them.
```

### Cue 9 · `overture:flow`

Target 16.5s. The close. Build across the sentences and then release completely on the last five words. Do not push the ending — let the music carry it out.

```
[Spoken Word]
[female narrator, soft, close, unhurried]

Stop working against yourself, and life plays as one thing.

Twelve things ticking apart cancel each other out. In step, nothing new is added — they simply stop cancelling. That is ease, and flow.
```

## Getting a take into the app

1. **Guide Voice → Your own voice → The opening**, then **Record these**.
2. **↑ Import** on the row whose id matches the cue. mp3, m4a, wav, ogg — under 12MB.
3. It is checked before it is stored: a file the browser cannot decode is refused with a
   message rather than saved and then silent when the line comes round.
4. Select **Your Own Voice** in the library. Any line you have not imported falls back to
   Solace, so a partial set is fine.
